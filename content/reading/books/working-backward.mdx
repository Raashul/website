Fun and easy read. I think there are some real valuable insights in the book. My favorites are:

1. Pizza teams - small teams are faster
2. No powerpoints in meetings
3. Six-Page narrative, PR/ FAQ - write in paper before starting to build
4. Work from first-principals
5. Be Customer obsessed
6. Separate inputs from outputs
