This book explores how Instagram grew into a cultural behemoth and details its high-tension $1 billion acquisition by Facebook while also focusing on the cultural clash between Instagram's founders and Mark Zuckerberg

Really loved the story and insight where the breakthrough for Instagram in the early days wasn't simply sharing photos but a simple photo filters that made ordinary smartphone pictures look beautiful, giving users the feeling they were great photographers.
